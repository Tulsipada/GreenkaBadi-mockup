Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 Aug 2026

What we collect

Name, phone, email, pickup addresses, scrap photos, and order history so we can schedule pickups and pay you correctly.

To run pickups, assign collectors, send status updates, process payments, and improve Green Kabadi. We do not sell your personal data.

Assigned collectors see what they need for your pickup (address, contact, item details). Support staff may access orders when you contact us.
