Terms of service

Last updated: 29 Aug 2026

Service

Green Kabadi arranges scrap pickup and pays based on verified weight and published rates at the time of pickup. In-app estimates are indications only.

Your responsibilities

Provide accurate item details and a reachable address. Keep prohibited or hazardous items out of pickups. Be available at the scheduled window or reschedule.

Payment

Final amount = verified weight x Green Kabadi rate at pickup. Payment method is agreed at collection (e.g. cash). Completed pickups show a payment receipt in the app.

Cancellations

You may cancel before the collector arrives. Repeated no-shows may limit future bookings.
