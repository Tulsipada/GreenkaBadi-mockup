Help & FAQ

How to post scrap?

Tap + Post Scrap, add photos, choose category, then confirm pickup address.

What materials are accepted?

Plastic, paper, metal, e-waste and other recyclables listed in the app catalog.

How does pickup work?

Admin assigns a collector. You get status updates: Waiting → Assigned → On the way → Completed.

Payments & receipts

Final pay uses verified weight × rate. Digital receipt appears in History for both you and admin.

Can I change my address?

Yes. Open Profile → Saved addresses, or Change from the Home address bar.

Still need help?

Our support team replies within 1–2 business days.
