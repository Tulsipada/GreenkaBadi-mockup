Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a pickup?

Download the GreenकBadi app, choose your material, add photos and address, then schedule a slot. We'll assign a collector.

How is the weight verified?

Your collector weighs the scrap on a digital scale at your doorstep. You can see the reading before payment.

Is there a minimum quantity?

A small minimum helps cover the trip. Exact minimums are shown in the app for your area and material.

When do I get paid?

Payment is made on the spot after weighing - cash or UPI - and a digital receipt is saved in your History.

Which areas do you cover?

Phase 1 is live in Siliguri. More cities will follow - watch the app for updates.

Is my address shared with everyone?

No. Your address is shared only with the collector assigned to your pickup.
