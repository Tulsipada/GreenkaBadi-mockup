About

About GreenकBadi

GreenकBadi is a technology-enabled recycling and scrap collection platform built to make scrap collection simple, convenient, transparent and organised.

Our connected platform brings customers, collectors and the Admin team together - from pickup requests and collector assignment to material verification, weighing, payment and digital transaction records.

Phase 1 starts in Siliguri, with Metal, Electronics, Paper and Plastic, and support for English, Hindi, Bengali and Nepali.
