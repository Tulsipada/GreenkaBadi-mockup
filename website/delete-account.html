Delete account · खाता हटाएँ

Last updated: 19 Sep 2026

Google Play and privacy rules require a clear way to delete your GreenकBadi account. You can delete from the app, or request deletion from this page if you no longer have access to the app.

This cannot be undone

Deleting your account removes your profile, saved addresses, chat history and in-app receipts. Complete or cancel any active pickups first.

1. Delete from the app (recommended)

  1. Open the GreenकBadi customer app and sign in
  2. Go to Profile → Settings
  3. Tap Delete account
  4. Type DELETE to confirm

2. Request deletion from this website

If you cannot open the app, email us. We process requests within 7 working days.

Request account deletion

Include your registered name, phone and email in the message.

3. What we delete

  • Profile (name, phone, email, photo)
  • Saved addresses and location pins
  • In-app chat messages
  • Open / cancelled order drafts
  • Login access

4. What we may keep

  • Completed payment / receipt records up to 24 months for accounting or disputes
  • Aggregated, non-identifying stats
  • Data required by Indian law for the legal retention period
