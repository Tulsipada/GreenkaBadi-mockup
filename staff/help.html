Help & FAQ

How do I start a job?

Open My jobs → job detail → mark On the way when you leave, then Complete pickup at the site.

How is final pay calculated?

Enter verified weight on the transaction screen. Pay = weight × current Green Kabadi rate.

Where is the Order ID?

On job detail and the payment screen. Share the short form with the customer if needed.

Where is the payment receipt?

After you complete payment, a receipt is generated for you and the customer. Both see the paid-to-customer amount. Reopen anytime from Done jobs.

How do I message the customer?

Open the job detail and tap the message icon. After the pickup is completed, chat and phone contact are closed — neither side can connect again.
